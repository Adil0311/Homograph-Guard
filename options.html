Homograph Guard

Browser extension for client-side real-time detection of IDN Homograph attacks and domain spoofing.

How the Defense Engine Works

  • ASCII Fast Path: Standard pure ASCII domains are instantly validated at zero risk with zero overhead.
  • Punycode Unmasking (RFC 3492): Translates xn-- encoded labels into their underlying Unicode glyphs to inspect hidden characters.
  • Mixed-Script Detection (Unicode UTS #39): Flags labels that interleave Latin letters with characters from foreign scripts (e.g., Cyrillic or Greek) — the primary vector for deceptive homograph phishing.
  • Confusables Table (142 characters, 5 scripts): Maps and visually unmasks lookalike characters (e.g., Cyrillic а imitating Latin a). Directly sourced from official Unicode confusables.txt (UTS #39 v18.0.0), providing full a–z coverage across the 5 primary impersonation scripts: Cyrillic, Greek, Armenian, Cherokee, and Georgian.

Privacy by Design

  • 100% Local Execution: Zero network requests, zero remote API calls.
  • Zero Telemetry: No tracking, no analytics, no persistent history storage.
  • The tabs permission is solely used to read the active tab URL when clicking the popup icon.
